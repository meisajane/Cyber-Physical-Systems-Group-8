# 3D-printed parts

The mechanism and the original part models are from **ZenXY v3 by V1 Engineering** (https://docs.v1e.com/zenxy/), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. We modified the design to run on the course kit's 28BYJ-48 motors and the parts we could source, and added new parts for the motor mounts, legs, wheel plates and pulleys. Our modified version is shared under the same licence.

Print settings: PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 20% infill, no supports. Print the pulleys at 0.12 mm layers so the GT2 teeth come out sharp.

| File | Qty | Function |
|---|---|---|
| `core.stl` | 1 | Magnet carriage; holds the magnet and clamps the belt |
| `cornermin.stl` | 1 | Rear-left corner; holds two idlers |
| `cornermax.stl` | 1 | Rear-right corner; holds two idlers |
| `motormin.stl` | 1 | Front-left corner; motor mount, idlers and both endstops |
| `motormax.stl` | 1 | Front-right corner; motor mount and idlers |
| `y_block_min.stl` | 1 | Left end of the X beam; rides on the left Y rail |
| `y_block_max.stl` | 1 | Right end of the X beam; rides on the right Y rail |
| `y-trigger.stl` | 1 | Blocks the Y endstop at the front of travel |
| `V_wheel_plate_3wheel_Yblock_x2.stl` | 2 | Wheel plate for each Y block, three V wheels |
| `V_wheel_plate_4wheel_Core_x1.stl` | 1 | Wheel plate for the Core, four V wheels |
| `Wheel_spacer_5x8x7.4_x10.stl` | 10 | One per wheel; sets the V groove at the rail edge |
| `Adapter_leg_28BYJ_left.stl` | 1 | Mounts a 28BYJ-48 under MotorMin and sets the frame height |
| `Adapter_leg_28BYJ_right.stl` | 1 | Same for MotorMax |
| `Rear_leg_left.stl` | 1 | Rear support leg under CornerMin |
| `Rear_leg_right.stl` | 1 | Rear support leg under CornerMax |
| `GT2_16T_long_hub_pulley_x2.stl` | 2 | 16-tooth GT2 pulley for the 28BYJ-48's Ø5 flatted shaft |
